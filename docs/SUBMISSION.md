## What this is

Assignment 4, Student Support & Ticket Management.

EduSupport is the Assignment 4 help desk. Students raise requests. Staff move them through a fixed status workflow. The SLA clock is separate from that status: a breach marks the ticket breached and escalated without pulling it out of In Progress. Pending time still counts toward the deadline. Managers see ageing, breaches, workload, and escalations.
